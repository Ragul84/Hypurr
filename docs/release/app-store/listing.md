**Name:** Hypurr

**Subtitle:** Coding agents from your phone

**Promotional text:**
Tell your coding agents what to build in plain words. Approve each step with one tap, see proof it works, and undo anything.

**Keywords:**
coding,agent,AI,developer,claude,codex,remote,terminal,git,approval,ssh,programming

**Description:**
Hypurr turns the coding agents on your computer into teammates you message from your iPhone.

Pick a bot, say what you need, put the phone away. You get a notification when it is done or needs your OK.

SAFE FOR ANYONE
• Every approval is one plain sentence with a risk level
• The agent stays in the folder you picked and never pushes to main
• Every step has a checkpoint you can roll back to with one tap

PROOF IT WORKS
• "Show me it works" runs your app and brings back screenshots
• "What changed" shows each file and the diff

WORKS WITH YOUR AGENTS
• Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, Gemini, Copilot and many more
• Group chats of bots, reply threads, bots that ask each other for help

FROM ANYWHERE
• End-to-end encrypted connection to your own computer (Windows, Mac or Linux)
• Remote screen, hands-free voice calls, Live Activity and widgets

Hypurr needs the free Hypurr host running on your computer. It is open source (MIT).
