Hypurr Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Hypurr ("we", "us") is published by Mindgains (mindgains.ai), India. Hypurr is a phone app plus a companion PC app that lets you run coding agents on your own computer and follow them from your phone. Contact: ragularvind84@gmail.com.

What stays on your devices

  • Your code and project files stay on your PC. Agents such as Claude Code or Codex run on your PC under your own accounts.
  • Pairing keys. Your phone and PC pair by scanning a QR code and talk to each other directly over your local network, end-to-end encrypted.
  • Camera is used only to scan the pairing QR code. Images are not stored or uploaded.
  • Screenshot text recognition (when you share a screenshot of an error) runs on the device; the image is not uploaded by us.

What we receive

  • Hypurr Free / Hypurr credits. If you use the built-in Hypurr Agent with Hypurr Free or credits, the messages you send to it (which can include code snippets from the folder you picked) are sent through our server at hypurr.mindgains.ai to third-party AI model providers to generate replies. Hypurr Free uses free models whose providers may keep and use prompts; do not use Hypurr Free for confidential or company code. Paid models use providers that do not train on your data.
  • Account and usage records. To count free messages and credits we store a pseudonymous (hashed) identifier for your PC and phone, message counts, token usage and credit balance. We do not store message content on our server after the reply is returned.
  • IP address is used transiently for abuse prevention and rate limiting (for example limiting new free trials per connection) and is not kept in our database.
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play and RevenueCat. We receive a purchase confirmation and an anonymous app user ID, never your card details.
  • Push notifications (when enabled) use Firebase Cloud Messaging, which issues a device token we use only to deliver notifications.

What we don't do

No advertising, no ad identifiers, no selling or sharing of personal data for marketing, no analytics or tracking SDKs.

Retention and deletion

Usage and credit records are kept while your account is active and deleted within 30 days of a request. To delete your data, email us from the app's "You" tab or at the address above with your request; uninstalling the apps removes all local data.

Security

All traffic is encrypted in transit (TLS or end-to-end encryption between your phone and PC).

Children

Hypurr is a developer tool and is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country).

Changes

We will update this page and the date above when this policy changes.
